Biography

I completed my high school education at Fuyang No.3 High School（阜阳市第三中学）. During this time, I was supported as a Pearl Student by the Xin Hua Education Foundation (XHEF)（新华爱心教育基金会）, receiving a total of RMB 7,500 to cover my living expenses. My school also waived my tuition fees. I am deeply grateful to my sponsor, Mr. Dunguo Mou (牟敦国, currently Deputy General Manager of Golden Eagle Asset Management), for his generous support. During high school, I was also introduced to Arduino and used it to build electronic projects, including a line-following robot.

From 2018 to 2022, I completed my undergraduate studies in Communication Engineering at the School of Computer and Information Engineering, Hefei University of Technology. Fascinated by robotics, I helped found the university's robotics team and participated in the RoboMaster Robotics Competition from 2019 onward. From 2020 to 2021, I served as Vice President of the university's Electronic Information Innovation Base（电子信息创新基地）, recruiting undergraduates from across the university who were interested in electronics projects and algorithms, organizing training, and coordinating participation in a wide range of electronics, algorithm, and robotics competitions.

I also served as Vice President of Hefei Pearl Family（合肥珍珠之家）from 2019 to 2020, organizing volunteer activities for university students across Anhui who had received support from the Xin Hua Education Foundation (XHEF)（新华爱心教育基金会） during high school. These included students from the University of Science and Technology of China, Hefei University of Technology, and Anhui University. Through this community, I also made many good friends.

From June 2022 to June 2023, I worked as a Research Assistant in the Autonomous and Multi-Agent Systems Research Lab (AMAS-Lab), Department of Electronic and Electrical Engineering, Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), supervised by Prof. Zhiyun Lin. After June 2023, I worked as a Research Assistant at the Intelligent Machine Research Lab (IMRL), SKL-IOTSC, University of Macau, supervised by Prof. Hui Kong.

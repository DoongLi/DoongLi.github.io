Research

Methods for robots in the real world

My research spans foundational methods and applications in robotics and 3D vision. I focus on making robots more capable in unstructured environments, from field settings to space exploration.

Space robotics

Autonomy, perception, and navigation for planetary exploration.

Field robotics

Robust systems for complex, changing environments.

SLAM & 3D vision

Mapping and scene understanding for embodied systems.

Active exploration

Methods that help robots choose where and how to explore.

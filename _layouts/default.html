Research

Leadership & Community Contributions

Dong Li actively contributes to the robotics and space robotics communities. He co-organized the Special Session on Robotics for Space Exploration at IROS 2026 and maintains awesome-NeRF-and-3DGS-SLAM , Active-SLAM-Paper-List , Awesome-Scene-Change-Detection , awesome-homography-estimation-and-image-alignment , All-3R-SLAM-in-this-Repo , ICRA2025-Paper-List , IROS2025-Paper-List . He also organizes WeChat groups to support Chinese researchers attending ICRA and IROS and encourage community exchange.
